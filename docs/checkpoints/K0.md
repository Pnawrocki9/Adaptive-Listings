## Defects found while measuring (not fixed here)

1. `scripts/dev/localhost-up.sh` step 8 trusts `.localhost-up/pids/ingest-8787.pid`: a pid file from
   a previous session pointing at a wrangler tree that is alive but no longer listening makes the
   step "leave it" and then fail the 120 s health wait. Remedy used: verify `/proc/<pid>/cmdline`,
   kill the old process group, delete the pid file, re-run with `SKIP_BUILD=1`. Candidate for a
   liveness check (port bound AND health 200) before "leaving" a pid — fold into FOLLOW-1246/1233
   (bring-up hygiene) rather than a new ticket.
2. `.al-local/al-real-down.sh` (Estalara repo) could not stop the frontend because its pgid file was
   stale; the Vite process had to be stopped by hand after cmdline verification.
